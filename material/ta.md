# Teaching assistant — Technical University of Denmark (DTU), 2024

Role: Teaching assistant on three MSc courses at DTU: 02456 Deep Learning, 02806 Social Data Analysis & Visualization, and 02805 Social Graphs & Interactions.

## What I did

- Mentored 150+ MSc students across the three courses: weekly exercise sessions, project supervision and grading.
- Deep Learning (02456): guided students through building and debugging neural networks in PyTorch, from training loops that would not converge to models that overfit in silence.
- Social Data Analysis & Visualization (02806): supervised hands-on projects on real-world datasets, with a focus on error-resistant data pipelines and honest visualizations.
- Social Graphs & Interactions (02805): supervised projects in community detection and recommendation systems.
- After the first assignment in Social Graphs, noticed that most students were making the same handful of mistakes and, together with another teaching assistant, ran a workshop to address them once, for everyone, instead of repeating the same feedback in each session.

## What the courses cover (current DTU course descriptions)

- 02456 Deep Learning (https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/02456): feed-forward, convolutional, sequential and Transformer networks, graph neural networks and back-propagation; students apply and analyse deep learning models, carry out a project in PyTorch, and write a short technical report. The first eight weeks are taught, the rest is project work.
- 02806 Social Data Analysis & Visualization (https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/02806): filtering, cleaning and organising large, complex datasets, and visualization for exploring single- and multi-variable data.
- 02806, continued: the limits of summary statistics (Simpson's paradox, Anscombe's quartet), Tufte's principles of graphical integrity, and building explanatory and narrative visualizations, for example with D3.
- 02805 Social Graphs & Interactions (https://kurser.dtu.dk/course/02805): collecting data through web APIs; natural language processing and sentiment detection in text; recommendation algorithms for media; network metrics, community detection and the dynamics of social networks.
