## Theses

- MSc thesis (DTU, 2024): Interpretable Sentiment Analysis with Concept Bottleneck Models. It has its own material file.
- BSc thesis (University of Copenhagen, 2019): Approach for Designing Moral Machine. I compared top-down (rule-based) and bottom-up (learned from data) approaches to building a machine that makes moral choices in trolley-problem dilemmas.
- For the bottom-up part of my BSc thesis I trained decision trees in Python on the public Moral Machine dataset (MIT Media Lab; 38 million rows): a depth-4 tree reached 69% accuracy and largely matched the global preferences reported in the original study, and an unconstrained tree reached 73%.
- In my BSc thesis I chose a white-box model deliberately and argued that algorithms with large social impact should stay comprehensible.
